## About me

I study Artificial Intelligence (B.Tech, 2023 to 2027) at NMIMS Mukesh Patel School of Technology Management & Engineering in Mumbai. I like taking a model out of the notebook and putting it behind something people can use: an API with auth, a dashboard, an app.

- **Now:** AlgoGuard, a trading engine whose risk rules the code enforces, so they hold even when a bad day says otherwise.
- **Before that:** SMG, a fraud classifier served by FastAPI with its own Next.js interface.
- **Also exploring:** an incident-analysis assistant that reads server logs with Llama 3 running locally.
- **Certified:** HarvardX CS50, Introduction to Computer Science (2024).

### [AlgoGuard](https://github.com/Vanshlikestocode/algoguard)
A rule-based trading engine for NSE equities with an enforceable risk layer. It asks one research question: does a pre-committed, automatically enforced risk layer cut tail risk, and what does it cost in returns? It includes backtests, paper trading through a broker layer, tests for look-ahead bias, and a Streamlit dashboard.

### [SMG Fraud Detection](https://github.com/Vanshlikestocode/SMG-Fraud-Detection)

The [Next.js interface](https://github.com/Vanshlikestocode/SMG-Fraud-UI) keeps the key on the server. **[Live demo](https://smg-fraud-ui.vercel.app)**
